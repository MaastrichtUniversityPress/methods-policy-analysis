# Welcome to Methods for Policy Analysis

In February 2006, a [Maastricht University](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/research/united-nations-university%E2%80%93merit) heeded a request from the International Labour Organization to start up a Master of Public Policy aimed at professionals who are at a career stage where they are becoming policy analysts and policy makers. The resulting [MSc Public Policy & Human Development](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/education/master/programmes/public-policy-and-human-development) offered now in partnership with the [United Nations University](https://merit.unu.edu) has served students from a broad variety of backgrounds. For now 20 years, the program has brought together recent Bachelor and Master graduates and mid-career professionals with training and work experience in diverse disciplines, and from many dozens of nations. Our students have always been united by a passion to serve their communities, and to pursue better public policies based on a rigorous analysis of carefully-collected evidence. 

It is the variety among our audience that has led us to write this book, when there are already so many wonderful resources on research methods in general, and each quantitative and qualitative method in particular. But after twenty years of methods training in public policy, we felt a need for a book that aims to be accessible to readers of very different levels of prior experience in research design and methodology. This includes students who have not had any training in either quantitative or qualitative methods, mature students who have not been academia for years and students who are engaging with this technical subject in a language that is not their own.

A more accessible textbook also means that its cases, examples and data need to be understood without assumptions rooted in high-income countries. Advice must be robust in data collection, cleaning and analysis. Policy analysis must be able to serve communities with unreliable infrastructure, essential vulnerabilities, and more substantive and urgent needs. We want to uphold the standards of evidence-based policy making under challenging conditions – because that is where good governance is needed most.

We have chosen to offer this open-access textbook in full recognition that we can do better to meet our own goals. We see this as an opportunity to contribute to the conversation, to provide a platform for future methods learning in public policy, and to be approachable and available to a diverse audience.

We hope that this textbook offers something of value to you – and that you will let us know where we can do better or dive deeper.

Welcome, dear reader! Thank you for visiting this source!<br/>
